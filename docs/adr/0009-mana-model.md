# ADR 0009 · Mana model: cap and king's reserve, three gauge sources, sleep/wake keeps the assignee

- Status: accepted · 2026-11-06 · updated 2026-11-06 with spike P1-02 (Claude Code 2.1.318) · amended 2026-11-07 (P1-05) · amended 2026-11-08 (P1-22)
- Related: [implementation-plan.md](../implementation-plan.md) §3.5, §9.1 items 8–9; [kingdom.schema.json](../schemas/kingdom.schema.json); [spike P1-02](../spikes/claude-2026-11.md)

## Context

Principle 6: two accounts (Claude Code; Antigravity), never rotated; the world uses up to the user's cap and always leaves the king's reserve. The product plan (§7) reads Claude `rate_limits` from status line data, but that is documented only for the status line, not for `-p`. Antigravity `agy -p /usage` (checked on this machine) prints remaining % and reset time per pool: "Gemini Models" and "Claude and GPT models", each with five-hour and weekly windows, plus a weekly cap for Gemini 4 Argon.

Spike P1-02 (Claude Code 2.1.318, claude.ai Team account): the status line never runs under `-p`. The same data reaches a headless session two other ways: each session's stream-json carries one `rate_limit_event` after its first API response (`unifiedWindows.five_hour` / `seven_day`: `utilization` 0–1, `resetsAt` epoch seconds, `status`), and `claude -p "/usage"` prints session, weekly and a per-model weekly line (`Current week (Fable)`) with no model call (`num_turns: 0`, cost 0). `modelUsage` in `result` has one entry per model, sub-agents included. No 429 happened; its signals stay unobserved until P1-16.

## Decision

- `kingdom.mana`: `cap_pct = 98`, `king_reserve_pct = 2`, `warn_margin_pct = 10`, `probe_interval_minutes = 15`. Threshold = `min(cap_pct, 100 − king_reserve_pct)`, computed per pool (`poolOf(tribe, model)`: the tribe id for a one-pool account such as `claude`; Antigravity: `gemini_models`, `claude_gpt_models`).
- `ManaGauge.read()` returns one `ManaReading` per (pool, window) with `source`: `rate_limits`, `estimate` (summed `modelUsage`/`usage` against `kingdom.tribes[].budget`), or `signal` (StopFailure `rate_limit`, repeated `api_retry` rate limits, quota errors).
- `rate_limits` for Claude: every session's `rate_limit_event` (`used_pct = utilization × 100`, `resets_at = resetsAt`), and `claude -p "/usage"` every `probe_interval_minutes` and after each session ends, parsed with a fixture-tested parser that falls back to `estimate`. Antigravity: `agy -p /usage`. A per-model weekly line binds only the characters on that model, like the Argon cap. (Amended 2026-11-06, P1-02, confirmed by the owner; implementation-plan §11.)
- `decideManaState` → `ok | warning | closed | asleep`. `closed`: no new quests, running work continues. `asleep`: the pool sleeps; running quests go to `paused_mana` after a WIP commit and keep their assignee. (Amended 2026-11-07, P1-05, confirmed by the owner: `decideManaState(readings, pool, cfg, now, model?)` drops readings whose window has reset, and a cap of one model binds only the characters on it; `closed` opens no new session of any kind; the readings reach the Steward as the runner signal `mana` and `ProbeResult.windows`.)
- While asleep the Steward runs the cheapest probe every `probe_interval_minutes` or at `resets_at`; success → `mana.restored`, the same character resumes (ADR 0017). An inbox `wake` probes at once. For Claude the probe reads `/usage` first (no mana) and makes the one-turn model call only when `/usage` says the window has room. (Amended 2026-11-06, P1-02, confirmed by the owner.) (Amended 2026-11-07, P1-05, confirmed by the owner: the probe comes one minute after a known reset, then every two minutes for ten minutes while the account resets late (`nextProbeOf`); awake pools are read with `probe({ping: false})`, no model call, in the background right after each session ends and every `probe_interval_minutes` while work is in play; the reading pace never holds work back. `elvaria wake <tribe>` sends `wake`.)
- (Amended 2026-11-08, P1-22, waiting for the owner: mana is counted per bucket the account reports, not per fixed pool. A gauge is a whole pool or the cap of one model in it (`PoolKey.model`: Argon, Fable); a pool binds the models the account lists in its group (agy: "Models within this group: …"), else those `POOL_MODEL_PREFIXES` gives it. A usage limit reads the account at once and sleeps only the gauges shown spent for the session's model, else the model's pool. A pool and window with no figure of the account's is estimated from the sessions' tokens, or minutes for a tribe without `usage` (`budget.minutes_5h/7d`); an estimate closes, never sleeps. A gauge warns once (`mana.warning`); the bell rings on warn, sleep and wake. `elvaria character model` moves a character to another model of its tribe.)
- `mana.reassign_after_hours` defaults to `null` (never); once set, the Steward gives a quest paused that long to a free character of its class whose own mana is open (P1-22). Elvaria never switches or rotates accounts; revival is the user's job.
- Scheduled jobs and probes count against mana and emit `mana.probe` / `mana.level`.

## Consequences

- The gauge works without status line data and gets sharper when a source exists. For Claude, `rate_limits` is now the main source; `/usage` text has the machine's time zone and no year, so `resetsAt` from the stream wins when both exist.
- One pool asleep does not stop the other.
- Still open after P1-02 (P1-16, the deliberate mana run): real `system/api_retry` and `StopFailure` `rate_limit`, `rate_limit_event` with `status` other than `allowed`, and calibrating `tribes[claude].budget` against `modelUsage` while no other session shares the account.
