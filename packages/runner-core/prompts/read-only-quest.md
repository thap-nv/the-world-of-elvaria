# A question, not a change

This quest asks a question: answer it without changing any file of the territory. You have no shell and no web, and your tools refuse every write but two files: your findings, in Markdown, to `{{report}}` at the root of your plot, and your handover letter. Where the answer calls for a change, describe the change in the report; do not make it. The Steward keeps the report on the quest's branch, which never leaves the kingdom.
